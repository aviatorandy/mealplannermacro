What the research says

Why 200 g, and what really drives weight loss
0.7-1.0 g per lb is the top of the studied range.

That equals 1.6-2.2 g/kg. A meta-analysis of 49 trials found muscle gains from extra protein leveled off around 0.73 g/lb (1.6 g/kg) in people lifting weights. Going to 1 g/lb adds margin while dieting.

Morton et al., Br J Sports Med 2018

With a lot of weight to lose, use goal weight.

Per-pound formulas based on total weight can overstate needs when body fat is high. Clinical reviews suggest goal, adjusted or lean weight instead. Enter your goal weight under Goals to see that range.

Protein requirement in obesity (review)

Power-food goals follow US dietary patterns.

At 2,000 kcal the US healthy pattern includes about 8 oz of seafood and 1.5 cups of beans a week, plus 2.5 cups of vegetables a day. The stickers turn those into weekly targets.

UC Davis summary of the Dietary Guidelines

Total calories still decide weight loss.

Higher protein helps keep muscle and fullness while dieting, but a meta-analysis of 13 trials found adding breakfast did not help weight loss. The daily calorie number is what moves the scale.

Leidy 2015 · Sievert 2019

Planning tool, not medical advice. High protein intake is generally considered safe for healthy kidneys; if you have kidney disease, diabetes or take diet-sensitive medication, check your targets with a doctor or registered dietitian.
